GeoBoard is an interactive learning platform that helps students explore how geography, history, and political systems change over time. It focuses on systems, tradeoffs, and cause-and-effect—not on telling users what to believe. It is not intended to be a replacement of teachers, families or peers, but is meant to assist students in their learning journey. Please note that this is a prototype to showcase GeoBoard and may contain bugs, AI filler text in articles or incomplete features.


Developed by Nathan Wong, a high school student in Vancouver, Canada.
