# Welcome to the GeoBoard!
**The world, at your fingertips.**

GeoBoard is an educational platform designed to make history, geography, and politics engaging and accessible for young people—regardless of ethnic, economic, or religious background.

These subjects are essential: they explain how the world has worked, illuminate how it functions today, and help us anticipate how it may change tomorrow.

GeoBoard is built on the belief that politically informed citizens, grounded in democratic values and human rights, are essential for reducing future corruption and incompetence in leadership—taking a meaningful step toward long-term global stability and peace.

Instead of memorizing dates from textbooks or passively consuming AI-generated summaries, students actively explore wars, revolutions, and treaties through interactive articles, visual storytelling, and simulations. Every design choice emphasizes **active learning**, encouraging curiosity, critical thinking, and a deeper understanding of the world.

---

## Why GeoBoard Exists

- Traditional education often prioritizes memorization over understanding, even though its content is credible
- Students disengage from complex global issues due to static or outdated teaching methods  
- Political literacy is increasingly shaped by low-quality AI slop or polarized social media platforms rather than informed exploration  

GeoBoard was created to turn these subjects into something students **explore**, not endure.

---

## Key Features

- 📚 **Interactive articles** organized in filters, tags, and thematic grouping. GeoBoard's articles explain **why** and **how** they happened, not just when, what and where.
- 🌍 **Visual-first explanations** of historical and political events to stimulate students and visualize hisotry better
- 🌓 **Dark mode & accessibility-focused UI**  
- 🧩 **Modular architecture** designed for future simulations and maps  
- ⚙️ **Versioned development** with active iteration and refinement
